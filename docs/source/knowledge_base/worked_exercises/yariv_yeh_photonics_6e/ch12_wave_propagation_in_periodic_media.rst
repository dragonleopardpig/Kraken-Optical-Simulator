Chapter 12: Wave Propagation in Periodic Media
==============================================

not reproduced.  Each entry supplies the governing model, a decisive solution
route, and an independent consistency check.

.. rubric:: Problem 12.1 — Bloch waves and multilayer transfer matrices: derivation

Begin with the governing equation named in the chapter and carry every algebraic or boundary-condition step explicitly; introduce approximations only after the exact relation is visible.
Multiply one unit-cell transfer matrix, use its unit determinant, and solve :math:`\cos(K\Lambda)=\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}M`.  Propagate :math:`M^N` or its eigenvectors to obtain bands and finite-stack reflectivity.
Pass bands require real Bloch wavevector; stop bands require exponential behavior, while power flux and the transfer determinant remain conserved in lossless layers.
