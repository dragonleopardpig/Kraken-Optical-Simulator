Section 5.1: Validity Of The Rate-Equation Model
------------------------------------------------

.. rubric:: Problem 5.1.1 — Harmonic response of a two-level atomic system

List the supplied quantities in one unit system, isolate the requested variable symbolically, and retain guard digits until the final evaluation.
Write one balance equation per level, :math:`\dot N_i=\sum_j(W_{ji}N_j-W_{ij}N_i)-N_i/\tau_i`, add population conservation, and solve the resulting linear steady-state system.
Check that every population is nonnegative, their sum is conserved, and the unpumped and strongly pumped limits are sensible.
