Section 18.1: Grating Diffraction And Scattering Effects
--------------------------------------------------------

.. rubric:: Problem 18.1.1 — Doppler shift from a moving grating

List the supplied quantities in one unit system, isolate the requested variable symbolically, and retain guard digits until the final evaluation.
Apply the grating phase-matching condition to the moving periodicity; differentiating its phase in time gives the frequency shift :math:`\Delta\omega=\mathbf K_g\!\cdot\!\mathbf v` with the diffraction-order sign.
Check that the shift reverses when either grating velocity or diffraction order reverses, and vanishes for a stationary grating.
